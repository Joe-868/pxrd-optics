What this calculation does and does not cover

  1. It is a geometric estimate of the full illuminated width, valid for line focus and a mask opening below 14 mm (workbook note).
  2. It applies when an incident Soller slit is installed; α is the acceptance angle of that slit.
  3. Removing the Soller slit is not the same as α = 0. α = 0 is the ideal parallel-ray limit. Without a Soller slit the width depends on the optics and cannot be taken from this model.
  4. It does not describe the intensity distribution or weak tails beyond W, so it cannot by itself guarantee clearance from a holder edge.
  5. For capillary transmission, the mask mainly limits the illuminated length along the capillary; keep W within the sample-filled section.
