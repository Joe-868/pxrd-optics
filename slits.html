Programmable divergence and anti-scatter slits

The incident programmable divergence slit (PDS) sets irradiated length. The detector-side programmable anti-scatter slit (PAS, called PASS in the MultiCore brochure) sets the observed region.

Where irradiation and observation overlap

Read down each column: PDS illuminates the sample, PASS selects what the detector can see, and their intersection contributes to the measurement. Each circle shows the same sample position.
