Beam geometry and sample illumination

The illuminated patch has a length along the beam and a width across it; compare both with the sample to see whether the beam stays on it.

Three quantities, two directions

QuantityDirectionMainly set byWhat it describes
Irradiated length LAlong the beam (scattering plane)Divergence slit opening and incident angle ωGeometric length between the limiting rays on the sample
Beam FWHM WAcross the beam (axial)Beam mask(s), Soller acceptance, sourceWidth at half the maximum intensity
Full axial footprint FAcross the beam (axial)Same as WFull width of the beam down to (near) zero intensity; always ≥ W

W shows where most of the intensity falls; F shows whether any of the beam leaves the sample. Neither includes very weak tails, which can still give holder reflections.

Does the illuminated patch fit a round sample?

A centred L × W patch fits a round sample of diameter D when its diagonal is no longer than D: L² + W² ≤ D². Use F in place of W for the full footprint.

Use the sample itself, not the holder: if the sample is smaller than the holder cavity, enter the sample diameter.
