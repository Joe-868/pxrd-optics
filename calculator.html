Explanations

Workbook-fit mask candidates

Pairs below pass the workbook W × L check at the current DS or target length. Select a pair to fill the iCore masks. This check does not show the full beam boundary or certify holder clearance.

How the workbook calculates these checks

Mask 1 and Mask 2 select FWHM width W from the workbook table. Fixed DS uses the workbook irradiated-length equation at the scan start. Automatic DS requests a length from 0.5 to 20 mm in 0.5 mm steps. The displayed DS settings are capped at Max (0.61°); above that limit, the iCore guide says the irradiated length decreases as the angle rises. Estimated L at stop uses the workbook geometry with the slit capped at 0.61°; it is an estimate, not a measured length. The guide's published critical-angle examples differ slightly from this workbook estimate.

The workbook compares L with √(D² − W²) for a centered, spinning round sample. Its automatic check also flags required DS above 0.61° at the scan stop. The workbook says its “Reduce” sample-fit warnings may be ignored when the sample is not spinning; that exception does not apply to the DS range check.

Sample height alignment and beam FWHM

In a direct-beam Z scan, move the sample surface into the beam until the transmitted intensity is about half its open-beam value, following the instrument alignment procedure. That height reference is distinct from W, the beam FWHM width across the sample in the mask table. Beam tails can extend beyond W. Alignment reference.

Why the dCore slit cannot erase every holder peak

The programmable anti-scatter slit limits what the detector observes; it does not change where the incident beam lands. A holder reflection along an accepted diffracted path may remain. Control incident illumination and verify with a blank-holder scan. MultiCore brochure.
