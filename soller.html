Incident Soller slit and axial divergence

The Soller channels transmit a finite range of axial angles, including slightly inclined rays. Whether a ray passes depends on its angle and where it enters the channel. Transmitted rays continue in the same direction; rays that intersect a plate are blocked. The drawing exaggerates the angles for clarity.

A tighter incident Soller slit accepts a smaller range of axial ray angles. The curves illustrate how the low-angle tail and peak intensity can change; their heights and widths are not calculated values. The workbook offers None, 0.02 rad and 0.04 rad. 0.06 rad is a teaching comparison only, not a selectable setting in this calculator.
